Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

WebXFly Web Agency ("WebXFly", "we") builds websites for businesses in India. This page explains what information we handle when you visit this website or contact us. We've kept it short because we collect very little.

What this website collects

Nothing about you personally. This website has no contact forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

It saves one small note in your browser's session storage so the opening animation doesn't replay every time you come back to the page during the same visit. That note never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you close the tab.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may process basic technical details — such as your IP address and browser type — in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Vercel's privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, call us, or email us, we receive whatever you choose to send — for example your name, phone number, email address and details about your project. We use this only to reply to you, prepare a quote, and deliver your project.

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share it with advertisers.
  • Messages sent through WhatsApp or Gmail are also handled under those services' own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them for your enquiry or project, and for any records we're required to keep by law.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you've shared with us. Just get in touch using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
